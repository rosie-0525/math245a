Low-dimensional cases

$\dim R = 1$
  • Vorst: $R$ is $K_2$-regular $\implies$ regular
  • Traverso: $K_1$-regular $\iff$ $K_0$-regular $\iff$ seminormal ($\iff$ Du Bois)
  • Weibel: $R$ is always $K_{-1}$-regular
$\dim R = 2$, normal ($\iff R_1 + S_2$ by Serre's criterion)
  • Weibel: $R$ is always $K_{-2}$-regular
  • $K_{-1}$-regular $\iff$ $K_0$-regular $\iff$ $H^1(E, \mathcal{I}/\mathcal{I}^n)=0$ for all $n\ge 2$, where $\mathcal{I}\subset \mathcal{O}_{\tilde X}$ is the ideal sheaf defining $E$ ($\iff$ Du Bois)
  • No known example of $K_1$-regular normal surface
