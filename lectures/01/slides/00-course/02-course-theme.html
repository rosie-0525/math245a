What this course is about

  1. Study both algebraic \(K\)-theory and Hodge theory.
  2. Explain a research program connecting the two subjects for singular varieties, emerging from the recent work of many people.
Hodge theory
mixed Hodge structures, differential forms
trace maps,
cyclic homology
Algebraic \(K\)-theory
$K_n(X)$, $KH_n(X)$
Prerequisite:
  • varieties, schemes, sheaf cohomology (e.g. Chapters II and III of Hartshorne);
  • working knowledge of spectral sequences and derived categories.
