Definition (Mustață-Olano-Popa-Witaszek, Jung-Kim-Saito-Yoon)

Let $X$ be a local complete intersection. We say $X$ is $m$-Du Bois if

$$\Omega^p_X \to \underline{\Omega}^p_X \text{ is a quasi-isomorphism for all } p \leq m.$$
Definition (Friedman-Laza)

We say $X$ is $m$-rational if

$$ \Omega^p_X \to \mathbf{R}\mathcal{H}om(\underline{\Omega}_X^{n-p}, \omega_X^\bullet)[-n] \text{ is a quasi-isomorphism for all } p \leq m, $$ where $n=\dim X$.
Theorem (Mustață-Olano-Popa-Witaszek, Jung-Kim-Saito-Yoon, Mustață-Popa, Saito, Chen-Dirks-Mustață-Olano)

Suppose $X$ is a local complete intersection of codimension $r$. Then $$X \text{ is } m\text{-Du Bois} \iff \tilde{\alpha}_X \geq m + r$$ $$X \text{ is } m\text{-rational} \iff \tilde{\alpha}_X > m + r$$ where $\tilde{\alpha}_X$ is the minimal exponent of $X$.
