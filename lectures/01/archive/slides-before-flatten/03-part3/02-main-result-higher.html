$K$-regularity and higher Du Bois singularities

Theorem (S.)

Let $X$ be an affine complex variety of dimension $d$, and $s = \dim X_{\mathrm{sing}}$. Then

  1. pre-$m$-DB $\Longrightarrow$ $K_t$-regularity for $t\le \min\{-d+2m+2, 0\}$.
  2. Suppose either of the following holds:
    • $X$ is a local complete intersection;
    • $\operatorname{depth} \mathcal{H}^0\underline{\Omega}_X^p \geq d-p$ for all $p \geq 0$.
    If $X$ is $K_{-d+2m+1+s}$-regular, then it is pre-$m$-DB.
Corollary
If $X$ is affine and a local complete intersection, then

$K_2\text{-regular} \implies$ pre- $m\text{-Du Bois for } m = \left\lfloor \frac{d-s+1}{2} \right\rfloor$ $\implies$ JKSY, MOPW,
MP, CDMO $\text{smooth}.$

Local complete intersection with $\dim X_{\mathrm{sing}} = 0$

Corollary

If $X$ is a local complete intersection with isolated singularities, then

$m$-Du Bois $\iff$ $K_{-d+2m+2}$-regular $\iff$ $K_{-d+2m+1}$-regular
$\iff$ JKSY, MOPW,
MP, CDMO 		$\tilde{\alpha}_X \geq m+r$

$$ \begin{array}{ccc} K_{-d+1}\text{-regular} & & \\ \Updownarrow & & \\ K_{-d+2}\text{-regular} & \iff & \text{Du Bois} \\ \Uparrow & & \\ K_{-d+3}\text{-regular} & & \\ \Updownarrow & & \\ K_{-d+4}\text{-regular} & \iff & 1\text{-Du Bois} \\ \Uparrow & & \\ \vdots & & \end{array} $$

$\dim X_{\mathrm{sing}} = 1$

$$ \begin{array}{ccc} K_{-d+1}\text{-regular} & & \\ \Uparrow & & \\ K_{-d+2}\text{-regular} & \iff & \text{Du Bois} \\ \Uparrow & & \\ K_{-d+3}\text{-regular} & & \\ \Uparrow & & \\ K_{-d+4}\text{-regular} & \iff & 1\text{-Du Bois} \\ \Uparrow & & \\ \vdots & & \end{array} $$

$\dim X_{\mathrm{sing}} = s$

$K_{-d+2m+s}\text{-regular} \implies m\text{-Du Bois} \implies K_{-d+2m+2}\text{-regular}$.
