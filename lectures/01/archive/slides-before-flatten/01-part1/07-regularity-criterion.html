Regularity criterion

Conjecture (Vorst)

If $X$ is affine and $K_{\dim X + 1}$-regular, then $X$ is regular.

Known for varieties over perfect fields by Cortiñas–Haesemeyer–Weibel and Kerz–Strunk–Tamme.

Theorem (S.)

For affine local complete intersections over a field of characteristic zero, $$\text{$K_2$-regular} \Rightarrow \text{regular}.$$

This is optimal:

Example

If $X$ is a simple normal crossing divisor, then $X$ is $K_1$-regular.
