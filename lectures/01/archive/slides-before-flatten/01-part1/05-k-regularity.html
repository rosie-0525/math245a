$K$-regularity

Definition (Bass)

We say $X$ is $K_m$-regular if $K_m(X)\cong K_m(X\times \mathbb{A}^r)$ for all $r\ge 0$.

regular $\implies K_m$-regular (Quillen)
$\implies K_{m-1}$-regular (Vorst, Dayton–Weibel)
Example

Let $X = (x^2{+}y^3{+}z^7 = 0) \subset \mathbb{C}^3$.

  • Not $K_0$-regular (Bloch–Murthy).
  • Not $K_{-1}$-regular (Weibel).
  • Is $K_{-2}$-regular (Weibel).
