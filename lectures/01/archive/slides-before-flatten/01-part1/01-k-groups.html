Algebraic $K$-groups

$$\text{ring } R \;\longmapsto\; \text{abelian groups } K_n(R),\quad n \in \mathbb{Z}$$

$K_0$
(Grothendieck) vector bundles on $\operatorname{Spec} R$
$K_1$
(Whitehead) automorphisms of vector bundles
$K_2$
(Milnor) relations among automorphisms
$K_3, K_4, \ldots$
(Quillen) "higher" structures

$K_0$ (Grothendieck group)
For finitely generated projective $R$-modules $P, Q$: $$K_0(R) \;=\; \text{formal linear combinations of } [P] \;\Big/\; \langle [P] + [Q] = [P \oplus Q] \rangle$$
$K_1$ (Whitehead group)
Let $GL(R) = \varinjlim\, GL_n(R)$ and $E(R) \trianglelefteq GL(R)$ the subgroup of elementary matrices. By the Whitehead lemma, $$K_1(R) \;=\; GL(R)\,/\,E(R) \;=\; GL(R)^{\mathrm{ab}}.$$
$K_2$ (Milnor)
$E(R)$ is perfect, so it has a universal central extension by the Steinberg group $\operatorname{St}(R)$: $$K_2(R) \;=\; \ker\!\bigl(\operatorname{St}(R) \twoheadrightarrow E(R)\bigr) \;=\; H_2\!\bigl(E(R),\;\mathbb{Z}\bigr).$$
$K_n$, $n \geq 1$ (Quillen's $+$ construction)
Apply the plus construction to $BGL(R)$ with respect to $E(R)$: $$K_n(R) \;=\; \pi_n\!\bigl(BGL(R)^+\bigr).$$ This recovers $K_1 = \pi_1$ and $K_2 = \pi_2$ and extends uniformly.
