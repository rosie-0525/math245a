Beyond local complete intersections

The conditions $\Omega_X^1 \to \underline{\Omega}_X^1$ is very restrictive!

Definition (S.-Venkatesh-Vo)

$X$ is pre-$m$-Du Bois if $\mathcal{H}^i(\underline{\Omega}^p_X) = 0$ for all $p \leq m$ and $i > 0$.

$X$ is pre-$m$-rational if $\mathcal{E}xt^{i-n}(\underline{\Omega}_X^{n-p}, \omega_X^\bullet)$ is a quasi-isomorphism for all $p \leq m$ and $i>0$, where $n=\dim X$.

Theorem (S.-Venkatesh-Vo)

If $X$ is normal, then \[ \text{pre-}m\text{-rational} \implies \text{pre-}m\text{-Du Bois}. \] However, $\text{pre-}m\text{-rational}$ does not imply $\text{pre-}(m-1)\text{-rational}$.

Remark

Imposing more conditions gives definition of $m$-Du Bois and $m$-rational singularities that:

  • agrees with the notions in the literature when $m=0$, or when $X$ is lci;
  • include more examples in general.
