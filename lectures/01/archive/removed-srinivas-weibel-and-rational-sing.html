Theorem (Srinivas, Weibel)

For a normal surface $\operatorname{Spec} R$, \[K_{-1}(R) \cong \bigoplus_{\mathfrak{m}\in R_{\mathrm{sing}}} \operatorname{Cl}(\widehat{R}_\mathfrak{m})/\operatorname{Cl}(R)\]

Example: Rational double points

Suppose $k$ has characteristic zero. $R$ has a rational double point if $\widehat{R} \cong k[[x,y,z]]/(f)$ for:

$(A_n)$ $f = z^{n+1} + xy$ $\operatorname{Cl}(\widehat{R}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/(n+1)$
$(D_n), n \geq 4$ $f = z^2 + xy^2 + x^{n-1}$ $\operatorname{Cl}(\widehat{R}) \cong \begin{cases} (\mathbb{Z}/2)^2 & \text{for } n \text{ even,} \\ \mathbb{Z}/4 & \text{for } n \text{ odd.} \end{cases}$
$(E_6)$ $f = z^2 + y^3 + x^4$ $\operatorname{Cl}(\widehat{R}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/3$
$(E_7)$ $f = z^2 + x^3y + y^3$ $\operatorname{Cl}(\widehat{R}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2$
$(E_8)$ $f = z^2 + x^3 + y^5$ $\operatorname{Cl}(\widehat{R}) = 0$

In particular, $K_{-1}(R)$ is torsion in these cases.

Recall that a normal variety $X$ has rational singularities if for a resolution of singularities $f: \tilde{X} \to X$, $R^i f_* \mathcal{O}_{\tilde{X}} = 0$ for all $i > 0$.

Theorem (Artin)

For surfaces in $\mathbb{A}^3$, $$\text{rational double points} = \text{rational singularities}.$$

Theorem

Let $X$ be a complex projective variety of dimension $d \geq 2$. If $X$ has rational singularities, then $$K_{-d}(X) \text{ and } K_{-d+1}(X) \text{ are torsion.}$$
