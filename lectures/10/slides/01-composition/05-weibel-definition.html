Exact categories: Weibel's definition

Definition (Weibel, K-book, II.7.0)
An exact category is a pair $(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{E})$, with $\mathcal{C}$ additive, such that $\mathcal{C}$ embeds as a full subcategory of an abelian category $\mathcal{A}$ with
  1. $\mathcal{E}$ = the sequences $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$ in $\mathcal{C}$ that are exact in $\mathcal{A}$;
  2. $\mathcal{C}$ is closed under extensions in $\mathcal{A}$: if $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$ is exact in $\mathcal{A}$ with $A, C \in \mathcal{C}$, then $B$ is isomorphic to an object of $\mathcal{C}$.
