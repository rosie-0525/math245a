Recall: Quillen's $Q$-construction

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an exact category. The category $Q\mathcal{C}$ has:

  • Objects: the objects of $\mathcal{C}$.
  • Morphisms $X \to Y$: isomorphism classes of diagrams $$X \overset{j}{\twoheadleftarrow} Y' \overset{i}{\rightarrowtail} Y, \qquad j \text{ admissible epi}, \quad i \text{ admissible mono}.$$ Two diagrams are isomorphic if there is an isomorphism $Y' \cong Y''$ compatible with the maps to $X$ and $Y$.
Question
How do we define composition of morphisms in $Q\mathcal{C}$?
