Applications Homotopy invariance and the fundamental theorem of $G$- and $K$-theory. For $R$ noetherian, $$G_i(R[t]) \cong G_i(R), \qquad G_i(R[t, t^{-1}]) \cong G_i(R) \oplus G_{i-1}(R),$$ and the same for $K_i$ when $R$ is regular ($K = G$ by resolution).

The Gersten spectral sequence and Gersten's conjecture: $$E_1^{p,q} = \bigoplus_{x \in X^{(p)}} K_{-p-q}(k(x)) \;\Rightarrow\; G_{-p-q}(X),$$ where $X^{(p)}$ is the set of points of codimension $p$.
